BANGLADESH NID OCR SERVICE - USER MANUAL
==========================================

WHAT THIS DOES
--------------
This tool reads a photo of a Bangladesh National ID (NID) card and automatically
pulls out the printed information (name, NID number, date of birth, address, etc.)
so you don't have to type it in by hand.

You give it a picture, it gives you back the text.

It works completely offline - no internet connection is needed once it is running,
and no data is sent outside the computer/server it runs on.


STEP 1: UPLOAD THE FRONT OF THE NID CARD
-----------------------------------------
1. On the /docs page, find and click "POST /nid/front".
2. Click the "Try it out" button (top right of that section).
3. Click "Choose File" and select the photo of the front of the NID card.
   - Accepted file types: .png, .jpg, .jpeg, .bmp, .tiff, .webp
4. Click the blue "Execute" button.
5. Scroll down to "Response body". You'll see the extracted information, for example:

   {
     "name": "MD SAKIB HASAN",
     "father_name": "MD ABDUL KARIM",
     "mother_name": "MST RAHIMA BEGUM",
     "spouse_name": null,
     "date_of_birth": "01 Jan 1995",
     "nid_number": "1234567890123"
   }

   If a field wasn't found on the card (e.g. no spouse listed), it will show as
   null (empty).


STEP 2: UPLOAD THE BACK OF THE NID CARD (OPTIONAL, SEPARATE STEP)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Find and click "POST /nid/back".
2. Click "Try it out", choose the back-side photo, then click "Execute".
3. The response will show:

   {
     "address": "Village: ..., District: ...",
     "blood_group": "B+",
     "issue_date": "01 Jan 2020",
     "place_of_birth": "Dhaka"
   }


TIPS FOR GOOD RESULTS
----------------------
- Use a well-lit, in-focus photo - avoid glare, shadows, or blur.
- Make sure the whole card is visible in the frame (not cropped).
- Hold the camera directly above the card, not at an angle.
- Higher resolution photos generally give more accurate results.
- Front and back are uploaded separately - one photo per request.
